RENOVATION

?

Contingency is a buffer, not a real cost yet — it covers what you can't see until you open the walls (hidden electrical, plumbing, structural issues, price rises, trades running over quote). 15-20% of the reno budget is the standard trade rule of thumb.

Holding period can't be known for certain, only planned for — base it on trade timeframes plus a buffer for delays, and add typical days-on-market for the suburb (ask a local agent). Reno projects usually run longer than planned, so lean generous.

The estimate button below fills these plus rates/insurance using rough rules of thumb based on price and reno budget — useful as a starting point, not a substitute for real quotes.

SALE

?

Resale value is always a projection, never a certainty. Use recent sold prices (not listings) for comparable renovated properties nearby, or ask a local agent for a written appraisal based on your planned scope. Use the conservative end of the range, not the best case — overestimating resale is the most common way flip numbers go wrong.
