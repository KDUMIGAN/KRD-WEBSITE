Strava usage

Strava connection

Let members use my Strava app

People you tick can connect through your Strava app instead of making their own. First raise your app's athlete limit on strava.com/settings/api (up to 10, including you). They can read your Client ID and Secret, and you all share your app's Strava limits. Unticking stops new connections; to cut someone off completely, also reset your Client Secret on Strava.

Data

Full backup saves everything: segments, KOM times, PRs, KOM holders, KOM lists, ignore list, rider profiles, settings, rides already read and your Client ID/Secret. It leaves out your Strava tokens, so after importing on another device just tap Authorise with Strava once. Keep this file to yourself. It holds your Client Secret and Strava data.

Importing merges into this device: newer KOM times win, and lists are combined.

Get started

A one-off setup, about 5 minutes. Your Strava details stay on this device only.

1
Create your own Strava API app
Open strava.com/settings/api (log in to Strava first) and fill in:
  • Application name: anything, e.g. Segments
  • Category: Training
  • Website:
  • Authorization Callback Domain: localhost
  • Upload any image as the icon. Strava won't save the app without one.
2
Copy your Client ID and Client Secret
They're shown on that same Strava page once the app is saved. Tap "show" to reveal the secret.
3
Connect to Strava
Tap Authorise and approve. The page it lands on will fail to load. That's expected. Copy the whole address from the address bar, paste it below and tap Connect.
Already have a refresh token?
4
Bring in your segments
Pick one or both. Then tap Get KOM times on the Ride tab.

Notes

Everything stays in this browser (local storage). Strava's API terms restrict this data to personal use, so don't publish the lists. Wind comes from Open-Meteo and the map from OpenStreetMap.
